# Vaultage Trademark Policy

Status: owner-approved pre-public policy as of 2026-05-20.

The Apache-2.0 license for the public Community source distribution grants
rights to use, copy, modify, and distribute the covered source code. It does not
grant trademark rights in the Vaultage name, logos, icons, signing credentials,
domains, app-store listings, or official release channels.

## Goals

This policy is intended to keep the open Community source usable while
making it clear which builds, services, and communications are official.

## Allowed Uses

You may use the Vaultage name to:

- Refer truthfully to the Vaultage project.
- Describe compatibility with Vaultage protocols, schemas, or file formats.
- Identify an unmodified copy of the public Community source distribution.
- State that your work is based on, derived from, or forked from Vaultage.

These uses must not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or official release status.

## Restricted Uses

Without prior written permission, you may not:

- Publish modified builds as "Vaultage" or "official Vaultage" builds.
- Use Vaultage logos, icons, app names, signing credentials, or confusingly
  similar branding for a fork, paid service, hosted/sync service, or extension.
- Register domains, package names, social accounts, or app-store listings that
  imply official Vaultage control.
- Use the Vaultage brand in connection with malware, credential theft, abusive
  automation, or services that misrepresent how secrets are stored or handled.

Forks and downstream distributions should use a distinct product name and may
state that they are based on Vaultage.

## Official Builds And Services

Official builds are only those distributed through release channels controlled
by the Vaultage project owner. Future paid Agent workflows, Services/provider
automation, managed OAuth, hosted/sync services, premium connectors, signing
credentials, and brand assets remain separate from the Apache-2.0 Community
source license unless they are explicitly released under their own license.

## Permission Requests

Trademark permissions are not granted during pre-release. Once public, requests
should go through the monitored project contact listed in `SECURITY.md` unless a
separate brand contact is published.
